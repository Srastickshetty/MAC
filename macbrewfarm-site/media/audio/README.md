# Background Music (BGM) for Video Reels

Place your studio MP3 audio file here:

**File name:** `lounge-bgm.mp3`  
**Location:** `media/audio/lounge-bgm.mp3`

### How It Works:
- The website's Video Theater will automatically detect and load this track.
- When visitors click the corner **"Sound Off / Sound On"** toggle, this track plays continuously in a loop across all 4 video reels.
- Includes automatic smooth volume fading when muting, unmuting, pausing, or scrolling the video section out of view.
